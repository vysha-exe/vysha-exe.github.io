About me

Hey, I'm Vysha!

I'm a Computer Science student at Vanderbilt University who enjoys building things at the intersection of software engineering, blockchain, and agentic AI.

I'm particularly interested in decentralized systems, financial infrastructure, and how technology can make complex processes simpler and more accessible. From building cross-border payment applications and experimenting with on-chain verification to developing AI-powered tools, I enjoy turning ambitious ideas into working products.

I'm also currently conducting computational neuroscience research at Vanderbilt's Bastos Lab, where I work with neural signal processing and machine learning to better understand patterns of brain activity. Beyond research, I've worked on cloud infrastructure, and I'm very interested in computer vision. I'm also actively involved in Vanderbilt's blockchain community through hackathons, technical projects, and events.

When I'm not debugging code or working on another project idea, you'll probably find me practicing karate, at the gym, or having unnecessarily long conversations with friends.
