Sancus

One USDC balance to pay suppliers, staff and families across borders, with an AI agent that never moves money without you.

UBC Hackathon, 2026 · team project

Live app Code

overview

Sancus is a stablecoin business account built on Circle's developer suite and the Arc testnet. A company holds one dollar balance in USDC and pays people in Thailand (PromptPay), Indonesia (BI-FAST), Brazil (Pix) or US bank accounts (ACH) from it. The user picks who and how much; Sancus quotes the live rate, picks the rail and settles, and the blockchain stays invisible.

what it does

  • Accounts that mirror real companies: HQ, country branches, employees and personal accounts, each with its own wallet, balance, limits and permissions.
  • An AI agent you can text or talk to ("pay Siam Packaging 5,000 baht for invoice INV-88") that looks things up and prepares each payment as a card you confirm.
  • Agent payments in both directions over x402: the agent buys live route data from its own capped wallet, and Sancus sells its route quotes to other agents.
  • An "Ask Sancus" voice call on every page, using ElevenLabs text-to-speech, that answers questions about the screen, navigates and prepares payments.

how it's built

  • Every agent action is an HMAC-signed, expiring proposal bound to the user and the exact request. Only the user's confirm, by tap or voice, runs it.
  • Next.js route handlers for payments, quotes, the agent loop and the proposal executor.
  • Circle developer-controlled wallets, App Kit, the Gateway unified balance and x402 nanopayments on Arc Testnet, with Supabase Postgres and row-level security.
  • A hackathon prototype on testnets: payout and card partners run in sandboxes or as clearly labeled simulations, with no real customer funds.
