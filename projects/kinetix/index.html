Kinetix

Physiotherapy patients practise their home exercises with live, on-camera form feedback.

Oracle HackTN, 2026 · team project

Live demo Code

overview

Kinetix helps patients practise their physical therapist's exercises between visits. Point a webcam at yourself and it counts reps, spots common mistakes and tells you how to fix them, on screen and out loud. The therapist sees progress and approves AI-drafted changes to the plan.

what it does

  • Live form checks with spoken coaching cues after every rep.
  • Session summaries with pain and effort check-ins.
  • A therapist view with a caseload, patient progress and an AI plan review where nothing reaches the patient without approval.
  • No progression is suggested while pain is above the therapist's limit.

how it's built

  • Pose tracking runs in the browser, so video never leaves the computer: 33 body landmarks become 26 joint angles.
  • A logistic-regression classifier trained on 1,700 physiotherapist-labeled reps flags common form errors.
  • Oracle Cloud speech for spoken coaching and Oracle Generative AI for session reports, behind a FastAPI backend.
  • My part: backend components and the cloud integrations, built with my team.
